## Who the plan is for
A few clear answers for families arranging cover from abroad.
- Who is eligible? Zimbabweans residing abroad. Coverage is available for both members residing abroad and in Zimbabwe.
- What if I want to add my beneficiaries in Zimbabwe? Members are covered using the local deluxe plan rates.
- Who pays for premiums? The principal member living abroad pays the premium, in the currency they earn in. South Africa is shown in ZAR, the SADC region in 8 local currencies, and worldwide cover in USD, GBP, EUR and more.
