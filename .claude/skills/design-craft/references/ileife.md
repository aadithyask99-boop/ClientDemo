# ILE IFE (facials studio, South London)

Folder: `ileife/`. Mode: **client mock** (the README says the serif was "taken from the mock"). Owner confirmation pending on how the mock was supplied.

## Signature idea

Blur to clarity. The preloader dissolves from a blur into the hero, and images and text sharpen as they enter. The page colour, nav, text and lines also ease between four tones as the section at mid-viewport changes (registered `@property` colours).

## Palette and type

- Plum `#2d2230`, cream `#f4ede3`, sand `#e2d5c5`, rose `#e9d6d5`.
- Cormorant Garamond (display, weight 300 to 400, upright only) with Manrope for body. A serif is deliberate for this client. No italic font files shipped.

## Rules this site set

- No labels above section titles (kept from Doves).
- Desktop-only effects that can be toggled live: `?panel`, `?fx=off`, or `?fx=glow,peek,magnet` (cursor glow on dark sections, a facial photo that drifts after the cursor over treatment rows, a small magnet pull on Book buttons). Building effects as switchable modules made review with the owner easy.
- Copy comes from the client's own site and Instagram; reviews are verbatim. Invented items (quiz wording, persona names) are flagged for approval.

## Open items recorded in the README

Her own portrait, real booking URL, address and hours, own photography replacing Pexels stock, approval of quiz wording.

## Weaker / watch

- Stock photography limits distinctiveness; the design leans on colour and motion to compensate.
