# Our own mechanisms, by content type

Use this when a section needs designing and the reference has no counterpart for it. Each line is something we have already built on one of our unique sites. Borrow the **mechanism**, then re-skin it in the reference's type, colour, spacing and motion; never lift the look. Details are in `<site>/README.md` and the site's case study in this folder.

**Intro and first impression**
- The loader is the headline, which then zooms into its place in the hero: portfolio.
- A wordmark alone on the page, then the hero builds in: more-coffee.
- Blur to clarity: the intro sharpens into the page, and the page tone eases between sections: ileife.
- A gate that starts the music (a record slides out of a sleeve): djeska.
- A short video intro that starts and ends on the page's own frames, so it lands as the finished page: halcyon.
- Doors that open when a thread unties: garden.

**Hero**
- A giant name that sits behind the object (mask cut from its silhouette): orra.
- An edge-to-edge wordmark over a black and white photo, with a pixel-block dissolve: djeska.
- Full-bleed photos crossfading with a slow zoom, calm: doves-uk.
- A 3D product that turns as you scroll (rendered frame sequence): more-coffee.

**A statement or paragraph**
- Words light up one by one as you scroll: halcyon (Statement), orra (studio paragraph).
- Pinned statement cards that swap as you scroll: more-coffee.

**Lists, and choosing among many items**
- Hairline rows with a hover bar: orra (homes table), ileife (facials with prices).
- The word under the pointer lights up and a photo trails the pointer: halcyon (services), djeska (rooms list).
- A roller or slider with the active item highlighted: more-coffee (flavour sliders).

**Story and timeline**
- One painted scene per step, alternating sides, a thread through the knots: garden.
- Numbered steps whose lines fill as you scroll: doves-uk.
- Pinned chapters revealed through ink patches: orra.
- A chart that draws itself as you scroll: more-coffee (the 3pm test).

**Numbers and proof**
- Circular badges that fill like liquid: more-coffee. Glass cards that tilt under the pointer and count up: orra.
- Awards and publications as plain hairline rows: orra.

**Testimonials**
- A custom slider where the active card inverts: more-coffee. Verbatim quotes kept short: ileife.

**People and team**
- A ring of photographs that settles into a half circle beside the figures: orra (needs real, consistent photos).

**Objects and products**
- Rendered 3D (three.js baked to frames): more-coffee. Glass props that drift with the pointer: halcyon.

**Before and after**
- A drag-to-compare slider: ileife.

**Footer and call to action**
- A giant wordmark: more-coffee, orra. Keycaps that fall with simple physics: halcyon. A sticky player bar: djeska. A pricing sheet that slides over the page: portfolio.

**Interaction ideas**
- Jelly hover (squash, tilt, bounce back): more-coffee. Magnetic buttons, a cursor glow, a photo that peeks after the cursor (switchable): ileife. Dots the pointer pushes aside: halcyon.

**Using this list well:** choose the mechanism that fits the content and the reference's language, not the one you like best. Do not use a mechanism the reference already uses more than twice. Check `fingerprints.md` so the result is not a sibling of an existing site. Add a line here whenever a new site invents a mechanism worth reusing.
