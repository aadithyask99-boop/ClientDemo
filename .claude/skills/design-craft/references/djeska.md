# DJ ESKA (DJ and events, Hampshire)

Folder: `djeska/`. Mode: **recreate the structure of a template** (the README names the reference: a Framer "Soundform" template). Built to show the client before his friend's site exists. Owner confirmation pending.

## Signature idea

A vinyl gate. The client's logo is the record sleeve; a record slides out on hover and click. "Click to open" is the only way in and also starts the music (the click is the user gesture for audio). Seen once per session.

## Structure carried from the reference

Edge-to-edge wordmark over a full-bleed photo, hairline lists in place of cards, a sticky mini player, a music section (photo left, title and current mix right, four mix covers), a photo that follows the cursor over a list.

## Palette and type

- Black `#0a0a09`, ink `#f2eee6`, gold `#d6ae52` taken from his logo. Gold only on the wordmark, pills and small accents.
- Archivo Variable (width axis; 125% width and weight 800 only on the wordmark, section titles 700) with Figtree for body.

## Rules this site set

- No italics, no em dashes, no labels above titles, **no card grids**: hairline rows, and packages as three columns split by lines.
- Sticky player starts at 20% volume and appears once the hero is behind you.
- Enquiry is a sentence form that opens a prefilled WhatsApp message; no backend.
- Phone: wordmark stacks to two lines, tap targets 44 px, no horizontal overflow.

## Honest placeholders

Hero and event photos generated from his Instagram photo (likeness needs his approval), two mixes are other artists' tracks, covers are CSS records, packages named but not priced. All listed in the README.

## Weaker / watch

- Quality depends on his real photography and mixes arriving.
