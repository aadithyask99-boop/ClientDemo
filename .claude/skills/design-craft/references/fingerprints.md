## Devices that already repeat (do not reuse by default)

- **Huge wordmark as the first-screen element:** Brew More, Orra, DJ ESKA, Portfolio.
- **Condensed all-caps statements:** Brew More, Halcyon, DJ ESKA.
- **A single italic or serif accent word in headings:** Garden, ILE IFE.
- **A loader or gate before the page:** Brew More, Garden, ILE IFE, DJ ESKA, Doves (demo), Portfolio.
- **Dark section after a light hero with a colour-changing sky or tone:** Halcyon, ILE IFE, Orra.
