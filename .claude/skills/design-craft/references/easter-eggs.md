# Easter eggs (the owner loves them)

Hidden, playful moments are part of what makes the owner's sites feel alive and unique. Every plan proposes **two or three easter eggs rooted in the client's subject**, and each is built, not just listed.

## What the owner's sites do (the precedent)

**Portfolio (`portfolio/`):**
- Flick a card hard (or tap it): a scene plays (a build log types, a pixel spider crawls an SEO checklist and a score counts to 100, enquiries pop in).
- Type `seo`: a made-up search page opens and the site climbs from 8th to 1st. Type `hello` or `hire`: the page scrolls to Connect.
- Hold Shift anywhere: the changing headline word spins through twenty adjectives and settles back.
- Right-click the headline: a one-item custom menu ("Say hello").
- Click "Remote worldwide": the globe spins and a bubble gives the time in a far city.
- Keep pushing at the bottom of the page: a pixel rocket builds and carries you to the top.
- The cursor itself is a character (leans as it moves, squashes on press, becomes a pointing hand over links), and a sleeping cursor.
- Cartoon hint bubbles point at eggs not yet found, at most six per visit.

**Garden (`garden/`):** swans burst into hearts; five hidden rings glint and unlock a reward; long-press the wax seal for a secret message; colour swatches play notes; night mode after 8 pm or by tapping the moon; the cursor is a gold needle.

**ILE IFE / DJ ESKA:** a photo that drifts after the cursor, a record that slides out of the sleeve.

## Rules that made them work

- **Small trigger, scene loaded only when found.** The trigger is a few lines; the scene is lazy-loaded, so the page stays fast.
- **Rooted in the client's subject**, never generic confetti. The egg says something true about the client.
- **Discoverable but not in the way:** hints for eggs not found, a cap per visit, nothing that blocks the page, easy to dismiss.
- **Touch equivalents:** every desktop trigger (hold Shift, right-click, type) has a phone version (long-press, five taps).
- **Sound only when the visitor deliberately touches something**, low volume.
- **Works without JavaScript and with reduced motion:** the eggs are extra, the page stands without them.
- **Do not copy the portfolio's eggs onto another client's site.** The mechanisms transfer, the content does not.

## Ideas by subject (to adapt, not paste)

- **Cars and dealers:** hold the horn key to rev; click the badge to cycle the car's colour; pull a mock handbrake at the bottom; a km counter that rolls when you scroll fast; headlamps that follow the cursor in a dark band.
- **Food and drink:** pour on click; steam rises on hover; a cup that fills as you scroll.
- **Hospitality and travel:** a postcard that flips; a window that changes from day to night by time of day.
- **Services and agencies:** type a keyword to see a made-up result; a cursor that becomes the client's tool.
