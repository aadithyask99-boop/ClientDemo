# The owner's taste (evidence, not opinion)

Built from sites the owner approved and things they said. Items marked *(inferred)* are my reading, confirmed by the owner only where stated. Read this before writing a plan, and give it to the critic agent (`.claude/agents/owner-critic.md`).

## What the owner wants (their words)
A very interactive, immersive, smooth, responsive website. Nothing generic, no boxy layouts, no gradients for decoration. Its own character, a video or 3D or interactive hero, very smooth motion, and easter eggs. The hero is what makes a site unique, and the first impression matters most. Sections with no emotion ("plain") are a failure even if every check passes.

## Likes (with evidence)
- **A hero that is a scene or an object, not a layout:** Halcyon's kingfisher film that becomes the page; Orra's text behind the object; the Garden's seal and doors; DJ ESKA's vinyl gate; More Coffee's rendered tub; motionsites picks: Liquid Glass Agency, Digital Experiences, Aethera Studio, Relocation Card, Atlas Nav, Lauberhorn Ridge, Unwind Hero.
- **Film or generated cinematic backgrounds** with very little UI over them; glass UI is fine when it is a few pieces that belong to the scene and carry real information (Halcyon, Lauberhorn Ridge).
- **Real 3D or rendered objects with character** (the tub, glass, keycaps), not stock photos.
- **Physical, smooth motion and surprises:** smooth scroll, pixel or blur reveals, a rope or thread that behaves, a cursor with character, easter eggs (the portfolio, the Garden).
- **A strong idea rooted in the client's subject** carrying the page.
- *(inferred)* Pinned stories with emotion (painting a face in four steps), image-wipe lists, full-bleed night or place photography, giant footer marks.

## Hard passes (stated)
- Boxy rows of same-size cards; decorative gradients; small labels above sections (eyebrows); hairline rows or rules; heavy headline walls; **gold or brass-yellow accents**; the same device repeated on every heading.
- Sections that are a small sentence in empty space, a heading plus a list, or plain boxes with numbers.
- A page that looks like another site's template, or a near-copy of the reference when the brief was inspiration.
- Pixelated or low-bitrate images and video; duplicated images; repeated sections; a double navigation.

## Unknown (do not assume; offer options)
Serif versus grotesk, dark versus light (approved sites go both ways), which accent colours beyond "not gold", how much motion is too much on a phone.

## How the owner judges
In one look at a finished page: does it have character, does any section feel plain or repeated, are the colours and type coherent, did it actually use the reference, do the banned devices appear. Counts and audits do not matter to them; feeling does.
