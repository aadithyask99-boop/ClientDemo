# Orra (architectural residences)

Folder: `orra/`. Mode: **inspiration for the hero, close study for the scroll page** (inferred). Hero is "inspired by the look of a luxury real-estate landing page"; below the hero is "modelled on the pacing and mechanics of an editorial architecture site", and the People section was "modelled frame by frame on the reference". Owner confirmation pending.

## Signature idea

A giant ORRA wordmark that sits **behind** a glass villa standing in still water. The solid letters are masked by the building's silhouette, and a thin outline copy shows faintly through the glass. One variable (`--night`) cross-fades a dusk photograph into the same scene at night with lights on.

## How it was made

- Two photographs of the same place generated and edited so building, position and reflection stay locked; a cut-out villa pasted onto a generated landscape then refined into one photograph.
- The silhouette PNG is never shown, only used as a mask. Everything is positioned in container units on a 16:9 stage so the scene stays registered at any shape.
- On phones and tall screens the picture's edges melt into flat colours sampled from the picture.

## Palette and type

Calm blue-grey paper, deep night blue, orange only as a small spark. Outfit for headings, Instrument Serif for statements and paragraphs.

## Scroll page (desktop with a pointer)

Hero stays pinned and softens as the page slides over it like a curtain; studio text read word by word; a table of homes whose hovered row becomes a dark bar; ink-patch reveals of pen sketches; an uneven tile grid drifting at different speeds; a principles ring that turns the page from white to night; a ring of eighteen photographs that settles into a half circle; partner names riding an oval.

Phones and touch get a lighter version of the same content: fades, slow strips, no pinning. The two versions are decided by pointer capability and width, not just width.

## What worked

- One strong depth trick (text behind the object) carries the whole hero.
- Interface stays quiet so the picture reads as a place; the scene itself does not move with the pointer.

## Weaker / watch

- Many pinned sections; each needs a deliberate phone fallback.
- Heavy dependence on generated architectural imagery; check realism at full size.
