# Sidecut (fictional London barbershop group), replica of an Instagram reel

- **Mode:** replicate-and-extend (owner: "I actually want to replicate"). Reference: a barbershop booking site shown in a reel (monochrome, wide grotesk, lit 3D chair). Studied from 41 full-resolution frames pulled from the reel's `<video>` by seeking and drawing to a canvas (Instagram shows a login modal over the page, so screenshots fail; canvas reads work). No live URL to run motion-probe on.
- **Owner decisions:** hypothetical barbershop with several branches in central London; hero option A (chair with a cursor-following light); a free 3D chair model was searched for and rejected (no CC0 barber chair found; "let's do our own way"); generated clip frames chosen over three.js.
- **Palette and type:** ground `#efeeea`, ink `#0b0b0c`, no accent (black, white, grey only). Unbounded variable (weights 200 to 800: light line plus bold line headlines, thin lowercase wordmark), Hanken Grotesk body, JetBrains Mono for tiny functional text.
- **Hero technique (cheap and fast):** one Seedream chair photo on black, then one 6 s 1080p Seedance 1.5 Pro image-to-video turn ($0.35). The model did a full, consistent turn with a locked camera. Every second frame is kept (73), cropped to the chair, 640 px webp (about 1 MB together). A canvas picks the frame from drag, scroll and a sway. The lamp is a CSS radial overlay following the pointer. No WebGL, no 3D library.
- **Assets:** 4 front and 4 back portraits (back made by editing the front as a reference, same person and lighting, $0.018 each), 4 props and a gift card on flat magenta keyed to alpha, hair texture for the intro. Total spend about $1.0 including retries.
- **Pipeline lessons:** (1) Node needs `NODE_USE_ENV_PROXY=1 NODE_EXTRA_CA_CERTS=/root/.ccr/ca-bundle.crt` to reach OpenRouter through the sandbox proxy, otherwise a 401 that looks like a missing key. (2) Magenta keyed backgrounds leave a purple cast on black and chrome; pull red and blue back toward green where both exceed it. (3) Ask the model for a plain dial: it printed BARBERSHOP on the clock the first time. (4) Generate the gift card straight on so HTML text can sit on it and CSS tilt gives the depth.
- **Bugs found by looking:** a CSS `translateY(110%)` start state is read by GSAP as pixels, so animating only `yPercent` left the hero title hidden (animate `y: 0` too); a CSS `transition: opacity` on elements GSAP also animates left the portraits at opacity 0.
- **Sections and mechanisms:** intro slash wipe, hero (drag and cursor light), price list (hover and stagger), props (drag with inertia), barbers (filter and hover flip), club (staggered plans), gift card (cursor tilt and spin), reviews (count up and stepper), shops, FAQ, booking drawer (four steps), footer (clipper pass). Easter eggs: type `fade`, clipper over the wordmark.
- **Impeccable:** real fix was grey text contrast (`--grey` darkened). Overridden: a "card in card" finding (pills inside plan cards and drawer options are deliberate), a false text-occlusion on the intro Skip button (the intro overlays the hero), and 1.6:1 on the decorative ghost wordmark.
