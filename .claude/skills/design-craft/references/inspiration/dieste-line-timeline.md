# Eladio Dieste: a line through time

- **Source:** https://www.eladiodieste.com/ (found via details.so: Timeline). Studied 2026-11-03 from 14 full-size strips; motion probed (105 elements move with scroll).
- **What it does:** a thin vertical line down the middle with a diamond node; years sit on the line and brighten as the node passes; photographs of different sizes sit left and right of it.
- **When to use it:** a short chronology (3 to 5 dates) where the order is the point.
- **How we used it (EVM):** a 1px line to the right of the year with ticks, a red fill that grows with scroll progress and a red diamond node; the year rolls digit by digit beside it.
- **Risks:** the collage is dense; keep one moment at a time. Do not let the node become decoration.
- **Approved by owner:** yes (plan v2, "go").
