# Virugroup: figures as white squares over a photograph

- **Source:** https://www.virugroup.com/en/nosotros/ (found via details.so: Stats). Studied 2026-11-03; motion probed (77 elements move with scroll).
- **What it does:** key figures ("50+ countries supplied", "10+ processing plants") as solid white squares over a full-bleed aerial photograph, large dark numerals, a short caption.
- **When to use it:** two to four proof figures for a company with a place-based subject.
- **How we used it (EVM):** three flush white squares (no shadow, no radius) over a Munnar photograph under a flat 30% scrim, drifting at different scroll speeds, with count-up figures; stacked without drift on phones.
- **Risks:** reads as boxy cards if there are more than three or they have shadows.
- **Approved by owner:** yes (plan v2, "go").
