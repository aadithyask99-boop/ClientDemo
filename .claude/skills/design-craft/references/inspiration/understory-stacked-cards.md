# Understory: three stacked step cards

- **Source:** https://understory.io/ (found via details.so: Steps, Features). Studied 2026-11-03 from 12 full-resolution strips; motion not yet measured.
- **What it does:** "Get ready for more bookings in three steps": three coloured cards (blue, purple, green), each with a step label, a title, a line of text and a UI mock-up, that stack on top of each other as you scroll.
- **When to use it:** the same short-sequence content as Daylight, in a more playful, product-led look.
- **How to re-skin:** only the sticky stacking mechanism is worth borrowing for a restrained reference; the coloured boxes are the boxy-card look to avoid. Replace boxes with full-width bands or type-led rows.
- **Risks:** reads as generic cards if the colours and boxes are copied.
- **Approved by owner:** no (lower fit).
