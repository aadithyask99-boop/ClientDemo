# Revelatio: people as a list with one portrait

- **Source:** https://revelatio.studio/about (found via details.so: Team). Studied 2026-11-03 from 14 full-size strips; motion probed (416 elements move with scroll, one canvas).
- **What it does:** the team is a hairline table (Name, Position, Location) with one large portrait at the left that belongs to the active row. Black text on white, no cards, no circles.
- **When to use it:** a short list of people (3 to 8) where one face at a time is enough.
- **How we used it (EVM, 2026-11-03):** four numbered rows in the right column (founder first), names at the reference's 40px statement size, one 200px greyscale portrait in the middle column that changes with the row nearest the screen centre or under the pointer; on phones each row carries its own small portrait.
- **Risks:** needs consistent photographs; with small web portraits keep the tile small and use greyscale and one crop to unify them.
- **Approved by owner:** yes (plan v3, "Go").
