Acme Plumbing: frequently asked questions
(This is made-up test content for a pretend client. Replace it for a real business.)

Who are you?
Acme Plumbing is a small plumbing and heating company serving homes and small businesses in and around London.

What are your opening hours?
We are open Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm, UK time. We are closed at weekends and on bank holidays.

Do you work out of hours or at weekends?
No. Outside opening hours the phone assistant takes a message and the team calls back at the start of the next working day. If it is an emergency, say so and the message is marked urgent.

What services do you offer?
Leak repairs, dripping or blocked taps, toilets and cisterns, blocked drains and sinks, radiators and boiler servicing, water heater checks, and fitting of taps, sinks and showers.

Do you do boiler installations?
We service and repair boilers. For a new boiler we give a free quote after a short visit. Ask to book a visit or leave a message and we will call you back.

How much does a call-out cost? What is the call-out fee, charge or price? How much do you charge to come out?
A standard call-out is 60 pounds, which includes the first half hour. After that labour is 45 pounds per hour plus parts. We quote before starting any bigger job.

Is there an emergency or urgent charge?
Urgent same-day visits during opening hours cost 30 pounds extra, subject to availability.

Which areas do you cover?
Greater London, within about 15 miles of the city centre. If you are further out, leave a message and we will tell you if we can come.

How long does a visit take?
Most visits are booked in 30 minute slots. The plumber will tell you if the job needs longer and will arrange a follow-up if so.

Do you give a guarantee? Is the work guaranteed? What warranty do you offer?
Yes. Repairs are guaranteed for 12 months. Parts carry the maker's own guarantee.

How can I pay? Do you take cash, card or bank transfer? What payment methods do you accept?
By card or bank transfer after the work is finished. We do not take cash.

Do I need to be at home?
Yes, or someone over 18 you trust. Please have clear access to the work area, and turn off the water at the stop tap if there is a serious leak.

What should I do for a serious leak before you arrive?
Turn off the water at the stop tap (usually under the kitchen sink or where the pipe enters the house), switch off electrics near the water if it is safe, and move valuables away.

Can I change or cancel an appointment?
Yes. Call us on this number and say you want to move or cancel your appointment. Please give us as much notice as you can.
