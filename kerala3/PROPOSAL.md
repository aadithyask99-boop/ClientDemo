## Against kerala3's ten sections
Already there, so not proposed: hero film and the four-tab idea (our hero cycles the places), Memories for a Lifetime (our dark "heart of Kerala is its people" and the sideways scroll), Gateway (stays and off the well-trodden path), Popular Destinations as a section (our pinned fan; only the list differs), What's New and Latest News (press releases and tenders are not visitor content), Connect with us (blank social embeds; goes in the footer), partner bodies (footer links).

## Five additions, ranked (revised after the owner-critic review). Recommendation: build all five, in this order.
The critic's test: which would we add if the official site did not have it? Wellness and the festival dates fit the spine (one year in Kerala) and fill real gaps. Films and Plan it are here because you named them; they are kept small and use no new mechanism.

### 1. Events: "After dark" (new section, after Get inspired)
- **What:** the dated festivals, tied to the October and November part of the year spine.
- **Recommended design:** one plain scroll scene. A full-bleed night photograph of the next festival (lamps and lit gopuram), its date set huge in the Red Dot serif with the green highlight on the day, and the other two festivals as two big lines under it. The motion is inside the photograph: lamps flicker, a slow push-in. No cursor effect (the page already uses the pointer in the hero).
- **Alternative:** the three festivals as three stacked full-bleed nights wiping by scroll.
- **Precedent:** the Doves full-bleed night image; Red Dot's growing full-bleed frame.
- **Not a repeat:** it is the only named-dates moment. It is dark, so it is placed after the paper Get inspired and before the paper Trips, away from the heart section.

### 2. Video Gallery: "Films" (small, after Stays)
- **What:** the site's video gallery.
- **Recommended design:** one large framed film (new footage: a snake boat race, Kathakali make-up, tea picking, not the hero's clips) that plays muted when it scrolls into view, with a plain link to the full gallery on keralatourism.org. No slices, no custom player.
- **Alternative:** drop it and link Media from the footer. Hero 4 already is a film and phones show posters only, so this is the weakest addition; I keep it because you named it.
- **Precedent:** Frostbound (a film carries a travel page).
- **Not a repeat:** one framed moving picture on paper, not a pointer scene.

### 3. Stunning Wonders: swap the content of Get inspired (existing slot, no new mechanism)
- **What:** the site's three headline themes are Religious Heritage, Malabar, Wellness. kerala3 has no wellness (Ayurveda is a leading reason foreign visitors come), no north Malabar and no sacred places beyond Theyyam.
- **Recommended design:** keep the three tall rising photographs; change the stories to temples and Theyyam, Malabar's forts and coast, an Ayurveda treatment. Kathakali and the weaver move into the trips section.
- **Alternative:** wellness as its own full-bleed photograph beside Water.
- **Not a repeat:** content only.

### 4. Hospitality and E-Brochures: "Plan it" (in the existing closing section)
- **What:** where to stay (Yathri Nivas eco lodges, information centres), tour operators, guides, and the e-brochures. The real site shows these as illustrated cards.
- **Recommended design:** the close keeps its single full-bleed photograph (a guide with travellers), with four links in the same plain style as the rest, each going to the official page. E-brochures are one of the four. The same links go in the footer. No brochure covers, no new scene.
- **Alternative:** footer only.
- **Not a repeat:** the closing section does not grow.

### 5. Popular Destinations: add Bekal to the fan (existing slot)
- **What:** the site lists Bekal, Kovalam, Thattekkad, Kumarakom and Kavvayi; the fan has none of the northern coast.
- **Recommended design:** add Bekal (fort on the sea) with its best months; the fan goes from seven to eight places.
- **Alternative:** swap Bekal for Varkala.
