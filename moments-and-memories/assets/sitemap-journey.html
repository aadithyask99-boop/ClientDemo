Revised sitemap and user journey: Moments and Memories

Today the site has 3 real pages (Home, Service, Contact us) plus sample and template pages that should not be public. The structure below is built around the customer's journey: find a service, see proof and a price, then ask for a quote in one step. Based on the audit, the journey map and a review of 6 similar sites (13 Oct 2026).

User journeys through the site

1Parent booking a kids' party

  1. Googles "bouncy castle hire Borehamwood" and lands on Kids' parties
  2. Sees real photos, "from £" price and reviews
  3. Checks FAQs and safety for insurance
  4. Taps Get a free quote (or WhatsApp / call)
  5. Thank you page, reply the same day

2Host planning a marquee or cocktail party

  1. Arrives on Services from search or social
  2. Opens Marquees or Cocktail bars, then Gallery
  3. Compares Packages and prices
  4. Taps Get a free quote with date and guests
  5. Quote by WhatsApp or email

3Local searcher or word-of-mouth visitor

  1. Finds the Google Business Profile or is sent the link by a friend
  2. Lands on Home or Areas we cover
  3. Confirms Lisa serves the area
  4. Reads reviews on Gallery and reviews
  5. Taps call or WhatsApp straight away

What changes from today

  • 3 pages become about 10 focused pages, each with one job
  • One page per service, so each can rank for its own search
  • Prices, proof and safety information are placed before the quote step
  • Quote page replaces the generic "Contact us" form as the main action; contact stays for call and WhatsApp
  • Sample and template pages removed from the public site

Why this structure (from the research)

  • The strongest competitor leads with quote, WhatsApp and phone on every page and navigates by event type
  • Two of four competitors show prices; local wording in titles and headings is common
  • The marketplaces make comparing suppliers easy, so the site must make deciding just as easy
  • Page order and names are a hypothesis. Confirm service priorities, prices and service area with Lisa before building
