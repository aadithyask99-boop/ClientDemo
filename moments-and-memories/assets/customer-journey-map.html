What the customer doesA birthday, engagement, wedding or milestone is coming. Needs a date and a rough plan.Googles "bouncy castle / marquee / cocktail bar hire + area", checks Maps, asks Facebook groups and friends.Opens several sites on a phone. Looks for real photos, reviews, prices, insurance and safety.Wants a fast answer. Taps to call or message, or fills a form with date, place and guests.Gets replies from a few suppliers. Checks what is included, delivery, deposit and who answered first.Wants clear terms, a simple way to pay, and confirmation that the date is held.Needs the supplier on time, equipment safe and the host free to enjoy the day.Posts photos, tells friends, may rebook next year (kids' birthdays repeat).
