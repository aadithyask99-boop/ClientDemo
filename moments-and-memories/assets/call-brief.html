Call plan and script

Moments and Memories · goal: Book the free 15-minute website check (screen share) and learn her real budget, goals and enquiry numbers. Not to sell on this call.

Opening (first 30 seconds)

"Hi Lisa, it's [name] from Maximus Mediascape. You filled in our form about digital marketing and lead generation, so I'm following up. Is now an OK time for five minutes? ... Before calling I had a look at your website and Google listing, and there are two or three small things I think could help. First, can I ask what you're hoping marketing will do for the business?"

Questions to ask (listen more than talk)

Her business

  • Which services bring in most of your work: kids' parties, marquees, cocktail bars, weddings?
  • What are your busiest months, and what are you booking now (Christmas, New Year)?
  • Which areas do you really cover?
  • About how many enquiries do you get a month, and which ones pay best?

How enquiries arrive

  • How do people find you today?
  • When an enquiry comes in, how do you reply and how fast?
  • About how many turn into bookings?
  • Do you get reviews, and where?

Goals and budget

  • What does growth mean for you in numbers (bookings a month)?
  • What have you tried so far (ads, social, directories)?
  • What would feel comfortable as a one-off amount, or a monthly one?
  • Who else decides?

Website and tools

  • Who built the website, and who has the hosting and domain logins?
  • Do you see how many people visit, or where they come from?
  • Are you on Facebook or Instagram for the business?
  • Do you have photos from real events and any reviews we could use?

Practical

  • Which address should customers see?
  • Do you have set prices or packages, and public liability insurance?
