# Moments and Memories Website Audit

- **Client:** Moments and Memories (Lisa Henderson, self-employed)
- **Site URL:** https://themomentsandmemories.co.uk/
- **Audit date:** 2026-10-13
- **Scope:** Visual/UX, mobile, conversion and copy, technical SEO, local SEO, performance, accessibility
- **Status:** Draft
- **Branch:** `moments-and-memories`
- **Source:** inbound lead from the lead-gen campaign. Wants digital marketing and lead gen ("My Market Share"). Small startup with no agency budget, wants aggressive growth. Contact details are held in the CRM, not in this repo.

## Files
- `findings.md`: the audit and recommendations
- `customer-journey.md`: customer journey map for party/event hire, with research and evidence levels
- `competitor-research.md`: what 6 similar sites do and what to borrow
- `gap-analysis-and-solution.md`: confirmed offer and prices, competitor tools, gaps, how we bridge them, structure for the call brief
- `journey.json`: data behind the journey map (rebuild with `_template/journey-map/build_journey.py`)
- `call-brief.json`: data behind the two-page call brief (`assets/call-brief.pdf`; rebuild with `_template/call-brief/build_brief.py`)
- `research-report.md` and `assets/research-report.pdf`: the full lead research dossier (rebuild with `_template/research-report/build_report.py`)
- `assets/`: screenshots, jev-seo report (`jev-seo-*`), Lighthouse reports (`lighthouse/`), Pa11y results (`pa11y/`)
