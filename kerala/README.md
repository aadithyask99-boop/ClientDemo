# Kerala, after dark (homepage concept)

A concept homepage for Kerala Tourism (keralatourism.org), in the design language of reddottours.com. Not the official site. Astro, plain CSS, GSAP and Lenis.

**Spine:** one night of Kathakali, dusk to dawn, lit by a single oil lamp. The cursor is the lamp flame; ink lifts to reveal each face and art.
**Hero:** ink-reveal Kathakali face (canvas ink curtain, then a lamp that follows the pointer).
**Palette:** cream `#F4EFE6`, lamp-black `#17110D`, kasavu gold `#A87A2C` (brighter `#C99A45` on dark).
**Type:** Cormorant (light) for display, Instrument Sans for text, Noto Serif Malayalam for the footer mark.

Content lives in `src/data/site.ts`. All copy is original; nothing is taken from the client's site.

## Placeholders and honesty
- Section links go to the keralatourism.org home page only; deeper links are not wired.
- Facts are stated plainly and should be confirmed by the client before launch (Pooram, kasavu, Koodiyattam age).
